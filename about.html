Our Story

Born in the Grow Room. Built for What Matters.

InnovoGrow was founded by growers and engineers who saw a better way to do lighting. We set out to solve the real challenges commercial cultivators face - heat, inefficiency, uneven coverage, and rising costs - by rethinking light distribution at the source.

Today, our solutions are trusted by cultivators across North America and beyond, from boutique facilities to large-scale commercial operations.
